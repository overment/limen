## Pi messages and lifecycle — report 1

- **1.1 — Accept: submission is falsely treated as acceptance.** Pi’s extension binding returns `void` and catches asynchronous rejection internally. The promise-returning rejection mock tests a contract Pi does not expose. Retained probes show steering moved to `delivered` with zero model calls, and a rejected wake remained claimed after authentication repair. Preserve pending evidence until observable consumption; do not fix this with `await`. Steering text survives, so this is false classification and lost automatic retry, not total data loss. [P `core/agent-session.ts:2574–2592`; L `hook/steering.ts:97–101`; E `pi/repro.log.txt`, `WAKE_REJECTED` and `STEER_REJECTED`.]

- **1.2 — Accept reload; narrow session replacement.** Public `session.reload()` produced `session-ended` although the session subsequently answered another prompt. Supervisor source makes that marker terminal. This establishes a broken hook contract, not observed live Herdr teardown. Reload must only clean up and reattach extensions. Whether `/new`, `/resume`, or `/fork` transfers or relinquishes the job requires an explicit contract; do not silently equate those operations with successful completion. [L `hook/hosted.ts:100–104`, `src/supervisor.ts:64–104`; P `core/agent-session.ts:2818–2841`; E `pi/repro.log.txt`, `HOSTED_RELOAD`.]

- **1.3 — Accept retry duplication; narrow “overrides aborts.”** A recovered provider error caused an unnecessary third call because Limen retained an earlier failure. An `aborted` result also caused resubmission with Pi retry disabled. The latter used a synthetic result, not a human pressing Escape. Persistent-error resubmission without a Limen ceiling follows the source. Let Pi own provider retries, evaluate the settled outcome, and retain an aborted delivery for explicit recovery rather than reopening it automatically. [L `hook/wake.ts:359–365,498–513,880–892`; E `pi/repro.log.txt`, `WAKE_RETRY_DUPLICATE` and `WAKE_ABORT_RESTART`.]

- **1.4 — Accept the unnecessary continuation, conditionally.** Real agent machinery made two synthetic model calls after `finish`; adding `terminate: true` reduced that to one while preserving the handoff. Interactive shutdown is source-traced, not terminal-tested. This flag suppresses the ordinary follow-up only when every result in the tool batch terminates; queued messages can still continue. Return it alongside shutdown, without promising unconditional immediate exit or adding tool-call policing. [P `packages/agent/src/agent-loop.ts:225–268,589–590`; installed `examples/extensions/structured-output.ts`; E `pi/repro.log.txt`, finish cases.]

- **1.5 — Accept identity failure; reject a drop-in custom-message cure.** A legal input transform caused two consumed wakes to remain unconfirmed. Structured identity is preferable to exact prose equality. However, `sendMessage` remains fire-and-forget and its triggering path bypasses ordinary prompt preparation—including Limen’s `before_agent_start` guidance injection. Any migration must preserve stable guidance even when a wake opens the first turn, not merely add a wake cue. [L `hook/wake.ts:490–496`; P `core/agent-session.ts:1182–1200,1482–1514`; E `pi/repro.log.txt`, `WAKE_TRANSFORM`.]

- **1.6 — Accept a missing wake-specific cue, not missing audience guidance generally.** The busy follow-up received no fresh “opened by a job wake” cue. Earlier communication context and the system prompt still existed; the probe does not demonstrate an incorrect response. Put one short origin cue in the notification itself. Do not duplicate the register on every delta or model call. [L `hook/communication.ts:41–55,101–109`; P `core/agent-session.ts:1209–1223`; E `pi/repro.log.txt`, `BUSY_WAKE_CUE`.]

- **1.7 — Reclassify as a review-policy decision.** Investigating regressions while reserving FAIL for acceptance failures is restrictive, but not logically contradictory: regressions can appear as notes. No unsafe merge was demonstrated. The specification needs to choose whether PASS means ticket compliance or broader candidate safety. I recommend allowing proven, candidate-caused contract and security regressions to block, including regressions introduced by a repair, while retaining bounded re-review and coordinator merge judgment. [L `templates/reviewer.md:8–20`.]

- **1.8 — Accept the false visibility assertion.** Pi explicitly renders partial content before an error or abort notice. That disproves “nothing reached the human” as a general inference, but does not establish what anyone actually read. Replace it with “the previous response failed or was incomplete”; correct the canonical prose as well as the generated cue. No additional steering rule is needed. [L `hook/communication.ts:58–64,104`; P `modes/interactive/components/assistant-message.ts:110–198`.]

## Implementation decisions

Retain files as durable authority and prose as judgment—not because both reports voted for them, but because these failures concern specific mechanical contracts. The smallest coherent directions are:

- **Truthful delivery:** distinguish submission, consumption, and settled response using observable Pi events. None proves that the coordinator reviewed or acted on the wake. Remove Limen’s competing provider-retry behavior.
- **Exclusive ownership and publication:** prevent checkout collisions and competing terminal writers. Remove destructive housekeeping where ownership is unknown; make interrupted mechanical claims inspectable and recoverable.
- **Safe control and evidence:** target owned processes or terminals, not labels or remembered locations. Preserve explicit handoffs separately from provisional snapshots.
- **Less incidental work and prose:** coalesce observations and correct the few misleading instructions. Do not convert this audit into acceptance parsers, product gates, repeated prompt inventories, or a workflow engine.

Herdr compatibility remains a separate decision. Installed **0.8.2’s client negotiated protocol 20**; the cited upstream declares **22**. The scratch probe did not query the installed server. Upstream terminal identity and background-start behavior are promising directions, not established installed capabilities. Do not remove focus compatibility code solely because upstream tests permit it. Workspace-label collisions and duplicate observations remain source-derived improvements, not additional reproduced failures.

Hosted-start ownership, route preflight, and reaper recovery belong with the existing boundaries named on the read-only board; history retirement is not the repair for unpublished-worktree deletion.
