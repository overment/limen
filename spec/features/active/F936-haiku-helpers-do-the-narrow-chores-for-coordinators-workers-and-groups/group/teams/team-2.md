# Team 2 · the signal fires at the moment of decision

Starting hypothesis: an agent forgets a routing table that it read an hour ago. The signal must arrive where the decision happens. Examples: a hint at spawn when the task has a scout shape, a wake for a failed job that suggests a triage helper, a check in the wake text that a helper packet has a `Verified:` line, a `group-peer` reminder after a team hypothesis is published, and a lead digest hint when cabinet events pile up. Every signal informs; none gates.

Test this hypothesis against the vision ("new machinery only where prose cannot reach"). Name the hook and the event for each signal, and cut every signal that prose already covers.

Your one worker does the whole brief: the delegation map, the sourced knowledge, the proposal, and the decision flow. Give it this note and the brief path in its task. You publish the checkpoints, answer team-1, and publish one team summary of 8 lines or fewer that names the worker's commit.
