# F073 · Picture pass after a shape moves

## Outcome

After work lands that changed the shape you carry in your head, a picture session rewrites one text diagram of how the system fits, where each feature stands, and the decision waiting on you. A routine slice earns nothing. The picture is readable in one sitting, in the same voice as any other reply to you, and it lives in Git.

## Scope

- One package prompt file: `templates/picture.md`. Spawn `--role picture --detached` after a merge, never blocking the next slice.
- Worth showing: the coordinator starts it only when they can name, in one clause in the handoff, a shape that moved — a job kind, a role, a process boundary, or where truth lives. If that clause will not form, they do not spawn. Prompt wording, tests, bugfixes, and ordinary slices are not a picture.
- One living file: `spec/picture.md`. A pass rewrites it. No dated diagram beside it, no per-merge file. Text-first (prose and, when a picture settles a structure, a fenced diagram). It reads the board, the feature folders, and Git; it never invents state.
- Held to the human register: nothing bare, nothing that needs this thread to make sense.
- Ship a history hash and extend the structure-test template list.

## Out of scope

- Starting a picture from a routine slice, or blocking merge or next work on the picture job.
- Generated image assets, or a gallery of old diagrams.
- Editing the board or opening tickets.

## Acceptance

- The shop manual states the after-merge detached trigger, the one-clause bar, and `spec/picture.md` as the only destination.
- `templates/picture.md` exists with a history hash; spawn `--role picture` loads it.
- The prompt forbids inventing state, forbids a second file, and requires the human register.
- A coordinator who cannot name the shape that moved does not spawn — the bar is in the shop manual, not in code.

## Notes

The decay mode is a diagram per merge. The bar and the single file are the whole defense.
