# F061 · The shop manual holds its ceilings

## Outcome

The coordinator honours a review appetite the owner states, ends a review loop at the second failed verdict, steers a wandering worker before restarting it, merges from a clean checkout, commits its own inline edits, turns a long dig into a survey job, keeps handoffs short, and carries a stated model choice onto every later spawn. At Alice the owner asked to skip reviews twelve times while ten more reviews were spawned, one slice took twelve review rounds, ten steers stood against seventy stops, two merges picked up unrelated drafts from a dirty tree, and fourteen replies asked whether anything had been committed.

## Scope

- `templates/agents.md` step 5 and 6: an appetite the owner states holds for the conversation; a second FAIL on a slice ends the loop and the reply names what remains and what it cost; a finding that widens the slice becomes a ticket line, not a repair; a re-review handoff names the findings file and the commit and stops, with no probe lists, no verdict words, no hand-typed hashes; the handoff names the one check that would prove the candidate wrong; `review-N.md` is committed on the candidate branch before the repair spawn, with trailing whitespace stripped on filing.
- Recovery: tool calls with zero changed files is a steer that names the file and the first edit; stop only after a steer is ignored; a steer to a worker that posted its handoff is a new spawn.
- Own hands: inline work ends committed or the reply says it is not; a dig past about five minutes becomes a survey job; the coordinator checkout is the only merge target and stays clean; visual work needs a render check or a spawn.
- Handoffs: single quotes or a file; over about two hundred words is a ticket problem; the seam is a file and the first edit in it; a stated model or reasoning level rides on every later spawn of that kind and is written into the board's decisions.
- A helper session never acts on a routed wake (F056); a finished hosted worker is finished (F055).

## Out of scope

- Any counter, field, or validator; these are sentences.
- The worker and reviewer prompts.
- Linear rituals.

## Acceptance

- The structure test asserts each added sentence by phrase.
- The manual stays under its current length plus ten percent.
- Every sentence traces to a numbered finding in the 2026-09-03 audit, listed in the folder's notes file.
