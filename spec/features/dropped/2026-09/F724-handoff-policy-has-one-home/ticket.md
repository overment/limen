# F724 · Handoff policy has one home

## Outcome

Handoff shape lives in the shop manual only. The speech register no longer restates constraint-per-line or a length ceiling. A coordinator reading both files gets one authority for that artifact.

## Scope

- Start at the Specs or Agent register sentences in `templates/communication.md` that mention handoff constraints or a length ceiling without owning the full rule.
- Keep the complete handoff rule in `templates/agents.md` (outcome, Ticket path, seam, one constraint per line, ~200 words, board boundary pointed at not restated).
- Remove the duplicate from the register. Update template history hashes.

## Out of scope

- Rewriting handoff practice.
- Stale board/vision “still ahead / NO-GO” prose (next slice).
- Changing spawn or review machinery.

## Acceptance

- `templates/communication.md` does not own a second handoff length or constraint rule.
- `templates/agents.md` still states the full handoff rule in one place.
- Template history hashes match. Typecheck clean.
