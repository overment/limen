# F084 · Every number arrives with its meaning

## Outcome

A reader who has not opened the project can follow any reply, because a feature
number never stands where a description belongs. Across 1,276 coordinator
replies to the owner at Alice, 2,828 feature numbers appear in prose and 126 of
them — four in a hundred — carry their meaning; 129 replies open with the number
as their first word and 282 make it the subject of a sentence. The rule is
already in the register with five worked examples, already in the per-turn cue,
and already in the specs reminder, and the board digest already supplies the
meanings for everything in NOW and NEXT. Placement, recall, and data are not the
problem: the cue states the rule as a property to satisfy, and a property reads
as satisfied.

## Scope

- `hook/communication.ts`: the reply cue names the failing case instead of the
  property. `No identifier without its meaning` becomes the shape the register
  already teaches — the bad sentence, then the good one.
- The specs reminder does the same for `no bare feature numbers`, which fails
  the same way in tickets at eleven in a hundred.
- `templates/communication.md`: the identifier section leads the Human register
  instead of sitting third. Its five examples are the authority the cue points
  at; they are not copied into the cue.

## Out of scope

- The register's other rules, shapes, and length.
- Any check, counter, or gate on generated prose.
- Measuring the ratio again after this lands, which would widen the quality role.

## Acceptance

- A human-audience turn attaches a cue whose reply rules contain a bad example
  and its replacement, not the word `without`.
- A turn after a ticket or board edit attaches a specs reminder in the same
  shape.
- `templates/communication.md` opens its Human register with the identifier
  section, asserted by phrase in the structure test.
- The register stays within its current length plus ten percent.
- The communication-hook and structure suites pass.
